## About

I work with founders and CTOs of small teams. One or two developers, often a designer, and an app in production or in the making. They want to go faster with AI without losing quality or control of their codebase.

I bring the app itself: web, iOS and Android from one React Native codebase. And a setup for working with AI, adapted to the project.

I built that setup on [Odisei Play](https://apps.apple.com/app/odisei-play/id6748117099), where I was the lead React Native engineer from 2024 to October 2026. We were two developers and a designer, on an iOS, Android and web app with more than 150 screens. The infrastructure is what held the pace.

Before code, I was an audiovisual technician. I retrained through OpenClassrooms in 2021 and I have kept learning since. Freelance for five years. I write about the method on my [blog](https://www.jean-desauw.fr/blog).

## 🧩 The AI setup

Two developers and a designer do not ship a release every two weeks unless the codebase does part of the work. That is what I install, as one package, adapted to your project.

- **A repo your agents can read.** One AGENTS.md that Claude Code, Codex and Cursor all read. Rule files that load only when the subject comes up. You change a convention once, every agent follows it.
- **A workflow proven over more than two years in production.** Hooks that hard-deny what an agent must never do: merge, push to a protected branch, run a migration against production. The specs, the reviews and the shipping decisions stay with your team.
- **A weekly report.** It crosses PostHog and your database. Your team sees what moved and what is going on in the codebase, then decides what's next.

At Odisei, the weekly report crossed PostHog, RevenueCat and Supabase. The team saw whether its work had moved retention and which bugs showed up in the logs. [How it works](https://www.jean-desauw.fr/services).
